Clinical Safety Gates — Current vs Required

The clinical workflow from intake to EMR population, showing 6 safety gaps (red) and the required improvements (green dashed). This is an outpatient clinic — emergency-severity symptoms should redirect to ER/911, not queue as bookings.

graph TD
    Submit([Patient Submits Intake]) --> EmergCheck{Emergency
severity?}

    EmergCheck -->|Yes| Fixed0[FIXED: ER/911 redirect
screen with Call 911 button]
    Fixed0 --> Queue

    EmergCheck -->|No| Gap1[GAP: No automatic
staff notification]
    Gap1 --> Queue[Booking in Queue]
    Should1[SHOULD: SMS/email alert
on red flag submissions]:::should
    Gap1 -.->|Required| Should1

    Queue --> Gap2[GAP: No priority sorting
by red flag severity]
    Gap2 --> Review[Staff Reviews Booking]

    Review --> Fixed3[FIXED: Booking linked to
questionnaire via FK]

    Fixed3 --> NurseQ[Nurse Opens Queue]
    NurseQ --> NurseRev[Nurse Reviews Session]

    NurseRev --> Fixed4[FIXED: Nurse review
enforced before doctor approval]

    Fixed4 --> Doctor[Doctor Approves]

    Doctor --> Fixed5[FIXED: max of questionnaire
+ AI urgency used]

    Fixed5 --> EMR([EMR Populated])
